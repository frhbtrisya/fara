My Work Experience 💼

Internship at Institut Sultan Iskandar

June 2025 - August 2025

My internship was short, only two months, but it was a very valuable experience. I learned a lot, especially because the tasks were related to my course in Information Management. During the internship, I gained practical skills and knowledge that I could not get from textbooks alone. It also helped me prepare for the real working world by teaching me how to handle office tasks, solve problems, and face unexpected challenges. Overall, this internship gave me confidence and a better understanding of how to work professionally in the future.

Part-time Onura Boutique

February 2025 - April 2025

I worked at a boutique selling Raya clothes at KL East Mall during my semester break. I worked long hours during the fasting month. It was tough, but I enjoyed it and managed the challenges. I learned to control my emotions and communicate well, even while feeling hungry and thirsty. I am proud of myself for handling everything successfully.

Part-time Raya Event

April 2023

I joined a Raya event in 2023 and worked the night shift from 7 PM to 7 AM for three days. The place was lively even at night, and I met many people with different behaviors while choosing Raya clothes. The crowd was amazing, and the booths were very impressive with famous brands. Even though I was tired and sleepy, I was happy and enjoyed the moment. This is an experience I will never forget.

Part-time Llaollao

August 2023 - September 2023

After SPM, I worked at Llaollao, a yogurt ice cream shop. I learned many kitchen skills, such as cutting fruits and making ice cream properly. My coworkers were very helpful and supportive. At first, I was shy and afraid to talk to people, but their guidance helped me become more confident. Working in F&B was challenging because I had to work quickly and carefully, but I enjoyed it and learned a lot.
